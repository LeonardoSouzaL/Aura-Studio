# Project Brief: Studio Aura Landing Page

## Project Overview
**Client:** Marina Vasconcelos (Studio Aura)
**Industry:** High-end Interior Design & Architecture
**Objective:** Create a sophisticated, immersive landing page that conveys "discreet luxury" and attracts high-net-worth clients in São Paulo.

## Brand Identity & Visual Strategy
*   **Aesthetic:** "Art Gallery" meets "Boutique Hotel". Minimalist, elegant, and immersive.
*   **Color Palette:** Deep charcoals and matte blacks (#121414) contrasted with luminous gold accents (#d4af37) for a high-end metallic feel.
*   **Typography:** Playfair Display (Serif) for headlines to evoke timeless elegance; minimalist Sans-serif for body copy to maintain modern legibility.
*   **Visual Language:** Focus on negative space, high-contrast imagery, and subtle gold linear elements.

## Content Structure & Requirements
1.  **Hero Section:** 
    *   Background: Immersive video (`interior-dark-video.mp4`).
    *   Headline: "Onde o Luxo Encontra a Alma" (Where Luxury Meets the Soul).
2.  **Process (3 Stages):** 
    *   Stages: Concept, Curation, Execution.
    *   Supporting Asset: `video-camadas.mp4`.
3.  **Portfolio:** 
    *   Curated gallery of 6-7 high-quality interior projects.
    *   Layout: Alternating grid or masonry to reflect a gallery feel.
4.  **About (The Visionary):** 
    *   Profile of Marina Vasconcelos.
    *   Story focus: Milan specialization, boutique hotel hospitality, and sensory experience.
5.  **Testimonials:** 
    *   Social proof from high-profile clients (CEO Tech, Art Collector, Architect).
6.  **Contact & Conversion:** 
    *   Minimalist contact form.
    *   Primary CTA: "Agende uma Consulta Exclusiva".
    *   Secondary CTA: Direct WhatsApp link for immediate inquiry.

## Technical Assets
*   **Videos:** `interior-dark-video.mp4`, `video-camadas.mp4`.
*   **Images:** 7 high-resolution interior shots.
*   **Design System:** Studio Aura (Custom dark theme with gold tokens).

## Success Metrics
*   Lead generation through the exclusive consultation form.
*   Direct inquiries via the integrated WhatsApp channel.
*   Reduced bounce rate through high-fidelity visual storytelling.
